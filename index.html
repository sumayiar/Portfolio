Roots And Inspiration

Family, Curiosity, and Grounded Design

I was born and raised in NYC, and that fast-moving environment shaped the way I think about software: it should be clear, useful, and able to meet people where they are.

I started coding as a child through Khan Academy web development exercises, and I was inspired by my father to study computer science because I watched technology evolve over the years from a young age.

My perspective is also grounded in a humble upbringing. I care about building experiences that feel warm, intuitive, and respectful of people's time and attention.

I live to lead, and for me leadership means bringing people together, creating momentum, and helping a team build something meaningful. Teamwork is the dream work.

Outside of coding, I love matcha, Central Park, nature, and hiking. Those parts of me influence the way I design and build: calm, intentional, and connected to the world beyond the screen.
